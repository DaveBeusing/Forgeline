# Bounded guided practice

Status: implemented.

## Context

The supplemental onboarding journal coalesces milestones by kind. Reading guidance and accepting commands do not establish practical mastery, and a coalesced entry can hide the identity of a particular receiving factory or unit.

## Decision and ownership

Register three explicit practice compositions over the compiled Central Divide composition. A distinct start-rule identity pins their deterministic initial fixtures. The runtime owns one PracticeSession and registers its normal paid setup system plus a completed-tick observer. Presentation receives a detached PracticeSnapshot and submits existing gameplay/session commands only.

PracticeSession retains at most four objective timestamps, four setup correlations and fixed targeted pending facts. It inspects at most 2,048 controllable entities and 256 combat events per tick. Initial placement considers at most 128 deposits and 121 candidates per building. Overflow or a missing observation interval fails explicitly. No full event history, arbitrary scripting, second inventory authority or renderer access to ECS is introduced.

GameplayProgressJournal.Recorded is an internal simulation-owner notification after a successful journal record and before per-kind display coalescing can replace another actor. Practice retains only targeted Ore receiver, later processing, authored truck load and authored squad transfer facts. Intermediate notifications cannot publish completion: the completed-tick observer alone certifies timestamps. Accepted requests, connections, reservations, initial inventory and card navigation cannot certify success.

Recon contact uses currently identified intelligence for the known public training fixture, with no hidden hostile transform read. Damage must be real and from an owned source. Other objectives inspect owned completed state or successful actual transfers. Captures are scoped to player 1, current session and exact completed tick; timestamp arrays are copied.

## Persistence and compatibility

Payload schema 12 adds optional PracticeProgress to saves. Ordinary saves must not carry it. Practice saves require it and validate bounded versioned metadata before reconstruction. Restore and replay reconstruct deterministic setup and progress from the existing command journal; restore compares derived objective timestamps/status/failure before returning the runtime. Snapshot session identity is regenerated. Cancellation follows existing restore disposal behavior.

No authoritative component or global gameplay rule changes. Envelope 1 and authoritative hash schema 18 remain unchanged. Practice fixtures change the registered start-rule identity; ordinary compiled maps, skirmish defaults, stock policies, AI and hybrid logistics retain their existing rules.

## Consequences and limits

The observer adds bounded per-tick practice work, not a measured performance improvement. Hiding guidance does not stop observation. Retry restarts the lesson; Skip returns to the menu. Destroyed critical owned units, impossible setup or lost observation produces a clear failure instead of fabricated progress.

The supply fixture demonstrates a real initially empty processing input and an empty field squad. It is a small reproducible recovery exercise, not a raid campaign or general mission engine. Native captures validate authored card states; deterministic command tests validate actual movement, construction, production, supply and save/replay outcomes separately.
