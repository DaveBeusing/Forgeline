# Predictive traffic — active finalization

The simulation now connects logistics traffic and continuous motion through GroundMovement. Each tick captures all current physical bodies, authored obstacles, precise service reservations and bounded per-vehicle neighborhoods. Missing, truncated, stale or exhausted captures hold controlled vehicles; they do not fall back to moving legacy proposals. GroundMovement remains the sole transform/order commit owner. Precise docking and queue movement keeps its original admission/release owner, with physical vetoes and swept envelopes protecting subsequent proposals in the same tick.

The batch planner uses canonical full identities and waiting age, sixteen acceleration/braking/yaw-limited prediction intervals, actual selected-route waypoints and authoritative terrain. Shared work exhaustion invalidates the entire batch before history commits. Confirmed free prefixes before future obstacles produce bounded braking speed rather than an immediate full-horizon hold. Every actual steering step is independently swept and arbitrated again. No new terrain, collision or stopping limits are waived.

Pinned-grid lane probes follow actual forward route segments. Copied directed passages and retained crossing envelopes provide exclusive admission. Occupied leases survive command changes, death and wrecks until the complete rear footprint clears. Unentered approaches expire after 80 ticks. Complete physical coverage is required for release. Bounds include 128 controlled vehicles, 4096 global bodies, 128 local bodies per actor, 512 historical actors, 128 leases and 262144 work units per tick.

A bounded wait graph selects at most one stopped reverse escape per cycle, including blockers owned by precise service movement. Initial escape verification simulates up to 128 reverse-steering intervals of .125 seconds and checks the complete arc, body geometry, reservations and other selected escape paths. A continuing escape retains its original command/goal and rechecks its next prefix; the actual fixed-tick commit independently checks acceleration, speed, yaw, fuel/tactical constraints and collision. A blocked route grants no translation. Ordinary replan requests use the existing search ownership path and preserve service generations.

Coarse motion retains real original orders, search IDs and start rotations. Far unentered reserved/busy/blocked docks do not take precise movement ownership. The exact slot and queue generations remain binding tokens. Runtime unit spawns use their authored terrain offset before capturing a route, preventing a rejected height jump at the precise-service handoff. Waypoint progress only advances from accepted unambiguous forward projection; a safe temporary turning arc retains its existing waypoint.

## Existing dock occupants and mixed physical cycles

A vehicle whose footprint already overlaps the reservation may evacuate strictly outward, segment by segment, without centre crossing, inward travel, rotation in place or reentry. Prediction, forward clearance, recovery previews and the actual post-arbitration step use the same bounded reservation predicate. Physical collision, staging, full-generation bindings and work limits remain mandatory.

Precise service owners now capture bounded immutable same-tick physical veto observations.
