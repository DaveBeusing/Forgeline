FORGELINE interface cues

navigation.wav, intent.wav, accepted.wav and rejected.wav are original mathematical
tone synthesis, with no recordings, samples or third-party sound libraries.
Reproducible source: build/Generate-UiAudio.py (Python standard library, build-time only).
Stereo 48 kHz PCM16, 35/55/95/110 ms; peak synthesis gain 0.16.
